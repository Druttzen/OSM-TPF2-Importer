Pulls highways, railways, landuse, water, places, signals, and mapped houses/shops/industry for the yellow box. Garages, sheds and untyped building=yes are skipped.

After convert, press Find & subscribe mods for this map. It reads osmdata.lua and matches Workshop packs to streets, tracks, tram, signals, airports, and so on. Region-specific packs (for example Swedish signals) are only suggested when the yellow box is in that region. Skip a pack and the importer uses vanilla TPF2 types.

Workshop mods

Subscribe via Steam only. Generic street/track/airport packs are listed for any map. Country packs are suggested only when the box is in that country. Missing packs fall back to vanilla TPF2 types.

Heightmap

16-bit grayscale PNG at the exact TPF2 pixel size for this map, cropped to the yellow line. Terrarium supplies the high-res surface; FABDEM (or Copernicus / SRTM) supplies bare-earth so forest canopy does not become fake hills.

Huge → 5121×5121. After import, type the Range min/max from the log into the map editor.

Satellite overlay

Esri World Imagery mosaic of the exact yellow box. TPF2 wants 4096×4096 DDS (BC1), vertically flipped — PNG tiles are written ready to convert.

Install & launch

Finds the TPF2 game, mods, Workshop, and heightmap folders from Steam, then copies the in-game tool into mods/osm_importer_1. Pause the game and open the OSM button on the menu bar.
